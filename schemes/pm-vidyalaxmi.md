# Scheme Overview: Pradhan Mantri Vidyalaxmi Scheme

PM-Vidyalaxmi is a landmark initiative approved by the Union Cabinet to ensure that no meritorious youth in India is denied higher education due to financial constraints. The scheme provides collateral-free and guarantor-free loans to students admitted into quality higher education institutions across India.

## Key Entitlements & Financial Realization
1. **Collateral-Free Loan**: Credit facilities up to ₹10,00,000 without requiring physical collateral or third-party guarantors.
2. **Government Interest Subvention (3%)**: For students with annual family income up to ₹8 Lakh, the Central Government provides an annual 3% interest subvention during the moratorium period (estimated benefit: ~₹60,000).
3. **Credit Guarantee (75%)**: 75% credit guarantee provided by the Government of India to scheduled commercial banks.

## Published Eligibility Gazette Guidelines
- **Target Institutions**: Must have secured admission into one of the top 860 HEIs ranked under the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).
- **Income Limit for Interest Subvention**: Family gross annual income must not exceed ₹8,00,000.
- **Credit Classification**: Strictly classified under DPI 2.0 as Subsidized Credit (Repayable loan with government subvention, not a cash grant).

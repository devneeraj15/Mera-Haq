# Scheme Overview: Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme. It offers a critical safety net against catastrophic medical expenses for vulnerable and low-income families across India.

## Key Entitlements & Financial Realization
1. **₹5 Lakh Annual Family Cover**: Cashless secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover of up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year.
2. **Empaneled Hospital Network**: Accepted at over 28,000 public and private empaneled hospitals across India.
3. **Pre & Post Hospitalization**: Covers 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization diagnostic and medicine expenses.

## Published Eligibility Gazette Guidelines
- **Target Beneficiary**: Households identified under SECC 2011 deprivation categories, or families holding state BPL ration cards with annual family income typically under ₹2,50,000.
- **Categorization**: Strict Cost-Avoided Welfare / Health Insurance Assurance.

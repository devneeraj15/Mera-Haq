# Scheme Overview: Pramod Mahajan Kaushalya Udyamita Vikas Abhiyan (PMKUVA)

The Pramod Mahajan Kaushalya Udyamita Vikas Abhiyan is the flagship skilling mission of the Government of Maharashtra, executed through the Maharashtra State Skill Development Society (MSSDS). It aims to bridge employability gaps by imparting state-of-the-art vocational and tech certifications.

## Key Entitlements & Financial Realization
1. **100% Free Training**: Free industry-aligned training worth up to ₹25,000 across engineering, IT, manufacturing, and emerging technology sectors.
2. **Direct Assessment & Certification**: State-recognized NSQF (National Skills Qualifications Framework) certificate upon course completion.
3. **Apprenticeship & Job Connect**: Automated linkage to state Rozgar Melas and apprenticeship portals.

## Published Eligibility Gazette Guidelines
- **Domicile**: Candidate must be a resident/domicile of Maharashtra.
- **Age**: Minimum 18 years, maximum 45 years.
- **Universal Opportunity**: Open to all communities, castes, and categories (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, Minority).

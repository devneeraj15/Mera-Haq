# Scheme Overview: Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj Tuition Fee Scholarship

The Government of Maharashtra provides financial assistance to students admitted through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) in approved government, government-aided, and private un-aided higher technical education colleges.

## Key Entitlements & Financial Realization
1. **50% Tuition & Exam Fee Waiver**: 50% waiver on tuition and examination fees (up to ₹50,000/year) reimbursed through DBT to the college/student account.
2. **Coverage**: Covers engineering, pharmacy, architecture, management, and technology courses approved by AICTE/DTE.

## Published Eligibility Gazette Guidelines
- **Domicile**: Must hold a valid Maharashtra state domicile certificate.
- **Income Ceiling**: Annual family income must not exceed ₹8,00,000.
- **Course Enrollment**: Must be admitted through CAP round in an approved institute.

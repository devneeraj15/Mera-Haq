# Scheme Overview: AI Technocrat at IIT Dharwad

Under the Pradhan Mantri Virasat Ka Samvardhan (PM-VIKAS) initiative of the Ministry of Minority Affairs, Government of India, the Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad (IIT Dharwad) offers a specialized 1-year residential training program in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing.

## Key Entitlements & Financial Realization
1. **Zero-Cost Technical Training**: Complete tuition and lab training worth ₹45,000 fully underwritten by the Central Government.
2. **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Stipend**: Monthly maintenance allowance of ₹1,000 credited directly to the citizen's Aadhaar-linked bank account (₹12,000 per annum).
3. **Campus Accommodation**: Subsidized hostel and dining facilities at the permanent campus of IIT Dharwad.
4. **Certification & Placement**: Co-branded certification from IIT Dharwad and the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), with institutional placement support.

## Published Eligibility Gazette Guidelines
- **Age**: Applicant must be between 18 and 45 years of age on the date of application.
- **Academic Background**: Must have completed a recognized Bachelor's degree (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc Computer Science, BCA, or equivalent degree).
- **Social Affiliation**: Must belong to one of the notified National Commission for Minorities communities: Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, or Parsi.
- **Selection Procedure**: Candidates qualify via an online aptitude screening test followed by technical interview rounds.
